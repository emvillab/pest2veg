About Pest2Veg

Pest2Veg is an educational web application designed to help UK growers identify common crop pests and learn how to manage them responsibly.

Supporting UN Sustainable Devlopment Goal 2

Pest2Veg supports SDG 2: Zero Hunger by helping growers identify crop pests and access information about responsible pest management.

Responsible Pest Management

Pest2Veg provides information to help growers understand common pests and consider responsible approaches to pest management.
