About Pest2Veg

Pest2Veg is an educational web application designed to help UK growers identify possible crop pests and access clear, practical pest information.

Our Purpose

Pest2Veg aims to make pest information easier to understand and access. Users can browse common UK crop pests or use crop and symptom information to find possible pest matches.

The information provided by Pest2Veg is intended for educational and informational purposes and should not be treated as a definitive pest diagnosis.

Supporting Sustainable Development Goal 2

Pest2Veg supports the principles of UN Sustainable Development Goal 2: Zero Hunger by promoting access to information that can help growers recognise crop pests and make informed crop protection decisions.

The project particularly relates to sustainable and resilient agricultural practices by encouraging pest awareness and responsible crop management.

How Pest2Veg Works

Pest2Veg allows users to select a crop and the symptoms they have observed. The application then compares those selections with verified pest information and displays possible matches.

Users can also browse the Pest Gallery to learn about common pests, affected crops, signs of damage and responsible management approaches.
