Identify common crop pests

Pest2Veg is a simple educational tool for UK growers. Choose a crop, describe the damage and where you can see it, then view possible pest matches.

How it works

1. Choose a crop

Select one of the six crops supported by this version.

2. Describe the damage

Choose the visible damage and where it appears.

3. View possible pests

Read information about pests that match your selections.

Important: Pest2Veg provides possible matches for educational purposes. It does not provide a definitive pest diagnosis.
