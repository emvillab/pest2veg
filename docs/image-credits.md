## Photographs we replaced after a visual check

Three images passed the licence check and the species check, then failed a
visual check: the team noticed they did not look like the other nine.

| Pest | Rejected image | Why it looked wrong | Replaced with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Thrips | Numbered figure plate from a scientific paper (panels 9 and 11) | A paper figure, not a photograph a grower would recognise | Colour photo of the insect on a flower, CC BY-SA 4.0 |
| Wireworms | 1900s line-drawing plate, black and white | Looked nothing like the rest, which were all colour photographs | Colour photo of the pale larva in soil, CC0 |
| Cabbage root fly | Fly isolated on a yellow flower | No crop in shot, so no context for a brassica pest | Fly on the young brassica it attacks, CC BY 4.0 |

None of these were caught by reading filenames or licence text. Only looking at
the rendered pages caught them. That is now a standing instruction: every
photograph gets an eyes-on check before it ships, and the contact sheet built by
scripts/build-photo-sheet.py exists for exactly that purpose.

## Rule we followed

An image is only used where its licence permits reuse, and a source that
publishes no licence is treated as not permitted, because silence is not
permission. A photo showing a different species to our record is rejected
even when the licence would allow it, because a plausible picture of the
wrong insect is worse than an honest illustration.
